# Anthropic Brand

Anthropic's official brand theme, built from its neutral earth-tone palette and editorial serif/sans pairing. Conveys a calm, grounded, and considered feel suitable for brand-aligned slides, docs, and reports.

## Color Palette
- **Dark**: `#141413` - primary text and dark surfaces
- **Light**: `#faf9f5` - background and light surfaces
- **Mid Gray**: `#b0aea5` - secondary text, muted UI elements
- **Light Gray**: `#e8e6dc` - borders, dividers, subtle fills
- **Orange**: `#d97757` - primary accent / call-to-action
- **Blue**: `#6a9bcc` - secondary accent / links
- **Green**: `#788c5d` - success / supporting accent

## Typography
- **Headers**: Poppins
- **Body Text**: Lora

## Best Used For
brand decks, corporate reports, product launches, editorial layouts, documentation, professional communications
